## Draft

**Announcing: Chrysalis Web Language (CWL) tip 1.0.26 is public.**

Most “web languages” you hear about are frameworks — React, Laravel, Next, and so on. They are great tools. They are also different *writings* of the same thing: what an app is allowed to do when someone hits a URL.

I built **CWL** for a simpler question: can we write down that identity in one place people can actually read?

CWL is a readable genome for a web app — Apache-2.0 under AgenticOp-io. Not a new React. Not a magic rewrite button. A clear inscription of:

• which routes and pages exist  
• what data they load  
• what UI they return  
• what effects they claim (auth, I/O, and so on)  
• and **honest holes** when we cannot claim something safely

That genome maps 1:1 to **WebIR** — the machine form of the same meaning. **Convert** (the Universal Translator) peels real origin stacks into that pair and emits modern targets. **Helix** (Secure) can optionally bridge the surface to traffic-proved DNA so you ask “is this still the app we certified?” instead of only “does this packet look weird?”

Neither Convert nor Helix gets to rewrite the language under a deadline. Models may propose. **Recorded traffic decides** what ships. Propose ≠ dispose. Label the holes. No façades.

Alongside the language release: in-depth technical papers on agenticop.io, a full published catalog of what I’ve opened, and a Zenodo archive so the papers are citable.

Three pillars:
• CWL — language of record  
• Convert — Universal Translator (including COBOL peels / pilots)  
• Secure — Helix traffic DNA firewall

Also public under AgenticOp-io: WPTP · Fragility Discovery Engine · Ghost Museum · Lane + PathfinderSSH MSP (upstream PathfinderSSH is Scott Peterman’s)
